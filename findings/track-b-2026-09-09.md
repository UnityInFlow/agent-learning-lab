## The four things this stop established, and the one it disproved

1. **A customization's claimed behaviour was checked by a script on every run, for the first time in
   this track.** `tools/check-phase-contract.py` reads the run's stream-json transcript and decides
   whether the phases were *followed* or merely *narrated*; its 15-case fixture set includes the
   retroactive-narration shape, and `tools/naive-phase-checker.py` is committed beside it as a
   negative control so the claim "the real checker catches what a text checker misses" is a runnable
   comparison rather than a sentence. Every earlier stop inferred behaviour from an answer.

2. **The deliberate failure closed the attribution and refuted itself in passing.** One variable
   removed — nine occurrences of `<<PHASE:X>>` reduced to zero, six phases left as prose — and
   markers went **10 of 10 → 0 of 3**. The prediction that the phase words would survive as narration
   was wrong: both checkers found zero. **Without the instruction to announce a phase, the model does
   not name the phases at all.**

3. **B5's stated purpose splits in half and only one half holds.** *"Prevent premature coding and
   false completion."* Premature coding: prevented, **20 of 20** treated runs across both tasks.
   False completion: **leaked, 4 of 20** — `DONE` emitted with all six markers and without its four
   contract fields, at the same 2-in-10 rate on each task independently. That is v1.1's brief, and
   it is the failure mode this stop can *name* rather than assume.

4. **Author decision 9's premise is confirmed and its remedy is one-quarter effective.** BE-003 has
   `architecture-consistency` at 2 on 20 of 20 and `maintainability` identical in both arms — 50 of
   100 rubric points with zero variance, which is why BE-004 was added. On BE-004
   `architecture-consistency` is again 2 on 20 of 20 and `maintainability` **floored at 0 on 20 of
   20** — the harder task closed that dimension rather than opening it. Only `test-quality` came
   alive. **Stop 13 must not choose its specialist skill from a failure that only those two
   dimensions report, because across 40 runs they have reported nothing.**

**What was disproved: the compounding-context mechanism.** Registered in P6 as *"one agent carries
one growing context through six phases, so every extra turn re-reads everything before it."* There
were no extra turns, and the arm that re-read everything was **the control** — a million cached
tokens per BE-004 run against the treated arm's half million. Refuted on two tasks here and in the
opposite direction by E-007 before that. **It should not be registered a fourth time without new
grounds.**

## Two process facts, both of which cost something

**Two builders were live at once and the pid lock could not see it.** The author started an
interactive session while `run-track-b.sh` was already driving print-mode ones on the same branch;
the lock guards driver-against-driver only. Caught from `pgrep` before the interactive session's
first edit and resolved by the author. **The in-flight BE-003 batch survived the kill because it
runs detached under its own `caffeinate`**, and was not restarted — §0 forbids re-running what
cannot be shown to have failed to start, and seven complete pairs were already on disk.

**Four runs were lost to a DNS outage and re-run rather than excluded.** `ENOTFOUND` in all four
logs, 12 KB logs against 150–250 KB, `taskAttempted: false`. The decision to re-run was committed
(`ea12b8d`) **before** the command was issued, and all four aborted runs keep their rows, logs,
worktrees and verdicts. `baseline-report.py` then turned out to classify them, on its own,
as *"discarded as harness failure (F13) — not agent behaviour"* — **the instrument already had the
category that neither experiment's registered exclusion list contained.** That gap is disclosed in
E-010 rather than back-filled.

## The single finding most likely to overturn this stop's result if pursued

**That the cost reduction belongs to *structure*, not to *phases*.** Both tasks show the treated arm
cheaper, and the mechanism that fits every number is that the control re-derives its plan on each
turn while the treated arm does not. **Nothing here separates "six declared phases" from "any stated
plan at all".** The deliberate-failure overlay — the six phases as prose with no announcement — is
the arm that would separate them, it exists on disk, and it was deliberately **not** read that way,
because reusing a control as a treatment for a question it was never registered against is the move
this project refuses. Registering it properly is a one-batch experiment and it would either localise
this stop's only real effect or dissolve it.
