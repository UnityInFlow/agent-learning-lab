## The finding of the stop is that "no control" and "a narrow control" are different failures

The same stop measured two corpora and the contrast is the result:

| | External-documentation extract | This machine's agent memory |
|---|---|---|
| corpus | 8 quotations, 1 page | 25 assertions, 15 memories |
| false today | 5 of 8 | 7 of 25 |
| control that executes | `check-links.sh` — **green**, `ok=3 broken=0`, exit 0 | **none**, proved by three searches returning zero matches |
| what it proves | every URL resolves | — |
| what it does not | that any sentence quoted off those URLs still appears there | — |

**Only the first one lies to you.** A green check over URLs invites a reader to infer a
guarantee about content that nothing ever made; an absent check invites no inference at all.
Collapsing the two into "neither corpus is checked" would lose the part that matters, so the
workbook keeps both sides and `GUARDRAILS.md`'s layer table gains the row *"not a layer"*.
